## Changes

Preserve alpha.1 steering, braking, drift, automatic acceleration, boost response and input recovery. Extend authored track to approximately 14.5 km, tuned to a representative clean completion between 240 and 300 active seconds. Keep the hard 300-second maximum and earlier terminal failures. Boost and better lines may produce faster skilled runs; no forced waiting stretches playtime.

Six distance-driven regions: Lockdown Avenue, Foundry Switchbacks, Broken Skyway, Freight Underpass, Siren Spiral and Last Exit. Add alternating tight bends, readable scenery/palette/structure changes, distinct musical intensity and exactly three mandatory gap jumps. Jump thresholds derive from gap geometry and a small safety margin; all can be cleared without boost. Use straight, level takeoff/landing sections and at least 300 m of visible approach. Two optional repair pickups restore 20 integrity, capped at 100.

## Pursuit

Interceptors approach from behind and fire a visible three-shot burst along a locked line after at least 1.2 s warning. The last 0.5 s does not track the player; moving sideways evades it. One burst can damage only once. Armored rammers approach alongside, warn for at least 1 s, then shove toward the committed line; braking behind or steering away evades them. Distinct vehicle silhouettes/colors, a rear scanner with actual distances, projectile/ram graphics and warning text make the enemies observable.

Enemies have real longitudinal/lateral positions, bounded acceleration, corner speed limits, barrier penalties and actual jump trajectories. No catch-meter damage or teleporting into attack range. Entry points are authored in distance order, with at least 2 s attack grace. Cap active pursuers at three and attack ownership at one. Cars left far behind retire; replacements enter only at authored encounters. Do not erase a clean driver’s lead continuously.

Suppress attack starts/resolve during tight curves and from at least 2 s before a jump through 1 s after landing. Remove dangerous in-flight shots on entering the protected corridor. Respect that protection regardless of frame rate. Enemy jumps can fail. AI travel and cooldowns freeze on pause/stall.

Initial tuning: projectile burst 10 integrity, ram 15, barrier 12; collect simultaneous damage proposals and apply the greatest once with a recovery window. Impacts and pursuit kills must identify their cause on results. Initial cruise/corner speeds, attack intervals and encounter spacing are tuning values, to be adjusted by measured runs without changing the accepted handling.
