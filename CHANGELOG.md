# Changelog

## v0.1.0-alpha.2 — Full escape and pursuit

Preserves approved alpha.1 handling and expands to 14.5 km across six themed sections, with 27 authored bends, three mandatory gaps, two repair pickups and a five-minute active-play limit. Adds shooting interceptors and armored rammers with real motion, readable locked attacks, capped concurrent enemies, protected jump windows, rear scanner and cause-of-defeat reporting. Extends original scenery and music by section.

The standalone file remains the mini-game playtest harness for the MathQuest cartridge. Narrative and math gates are not added in this milestone. Simulation, full browser keyboard run, synthetic input and instrumented audio checks are recorded in docs/VERIFICATION.md. Physical iPhone/iPad testing and deployment remain pending.

## v0.1.0-alpha.1 — Driving prototype

First playable mini-game test harness for the BLACKLINE MathQuest cartridge. Adds a short industrial test track, hills, five bends, brake/drift, boost, a gap jump, integrity/damage and pause/retry. Includes original preliminary canvas art, synth/engine audio, keyboard/touch input and scoped input-recovery handling.

Corrected the gap drawing after visual inspection, made its launch/landing elevations consistent, and allowed the result jingle to complete before audio suspension.

Ten simulation tests and the desktop/synthetic mobile/audio checks recorded in docs/VERIFICATION.md passed. Physical iPhone/iPad checks, subjective playtesting and deployment remain pending. Pursuit, full course, narrative and math gates are later milestones.
